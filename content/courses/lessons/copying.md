# How to copy

"Copying" is writing down what you hear. A few habits make it much easier:

- **Keep going.** If you miss a character, leave it out and catch the next one. Stopping to think about the one you missed costs you the next three.
- **Type it as it comes.** Don't wait for a group to end.
- **Relax.** Tension makes everything sound faster. It's fine to get things wrong; that's how the course learns what to give you more of.

The groups are random on purpose: there are no words to guess, so you're training your ear, not your vocabulary.
