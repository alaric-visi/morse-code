# Farnsworth spacing

If characters are sent at 20 words per minute, how can a beginner keep up? By stretching the **gaps between characters** while leaving the characters themselves at full speed. That's **Farnsworth spacing**, named after Donald R. "Russ" Farnsworth, W6TTB.

The character speed teaches your ear the right rhythm. The extra space gives your brain time to name what it heard. As you improve, you shrink the gaps (raise the *effective speed*) until there's nothing left to shrink.

You can change both speeds in the course settings. A good start is characters at **20 WPM**, overall at **10 WPM**.
