# How this course works

This is the **Koch method**, worked out by the German psychologist Ludwig Koch in the 1930s.

- You start with just **two characters**, K and M, at full speed.
- You practise copying random groups of them until you get **90% right**.
- Then a new character is added, and you do it again.

Because you only ever have a handful of sounds to tell apart, you're never lost, and every lesson is a little harder than the last. After about 40 steps you'll know the whole code.

Each lesson has four parts: **meet** the new character, **recognise** it among the others, **copy** a minute of random groups, and **review** whatever's due. Short daily sessions work much better than long weekly ones.
