# Listen, don't count

Every character in Morse has its own **rhythm**, the way a word has its own sound. *dah-di-dah* is K. You'll recognise it the same way you recognise your name being called, without spelling it out.

People who learn from a chart of dots and dashes end up counting: "dah, dit, dah… that's three, so… K". It works at 5 words a minute and stops working at about 10, because the sound arrives faster than you can count it. Unlearning the habit is much harder than never forming it.

So in this course:

- Characters are always sent at **full speed**, so you can't count them.
- You'll see the rhythm written as syllables (*di-dah*), never as a row of dots.
- When you don't know a character, let it go and listen for the next one.
