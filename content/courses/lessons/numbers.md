# Numbers

Numbers follow a pattern: each is five elements long, and the dits and dahs slide across as the number goes up. **5** is five dits; **6** is a dah then four dits.

It's tempting to work numbers out from the pattern. Don't! Learn each one as a sound, just like letters. On the air you'll also hear **cut numbers**, short forms used in contests, where **N** stands for 9 and **T** for 0. They come later in the course.
