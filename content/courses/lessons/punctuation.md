# Punctuation and procedure

The full stop you just met is *di-dah-di-dah-di-dah*. On the air you'll hear it less than you'd think: operators often send **=** (*dah-di-di-di-dah*, also called **BT**) as a break between thoughts instead.

Some characters double as **prosigns**: short procedure signals sent as one run-together character. **+** is the same signal as **AR** (end of message). You'll meet more of them later.
