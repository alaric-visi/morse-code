# SOS

**SOS** isn't three letters: it's one continuous signal, *di-di-di-dah-dah-dah-di-di-di*, chosen because it's unmistakable. It was adopted as the international distress signal at the **1906 Berlin radiotelegraph conference** and came into force in 1908.

British ships had used **CQD** before that, and the old habit lingered: in April **1912**, the wireless operators on the *Titanic* sent both CQD and SOS.
