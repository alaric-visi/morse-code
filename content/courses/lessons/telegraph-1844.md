# What hath God wrought

On **24 May 1844**, Samuel Morse sent the message *"What hath God wrought"* from the US Capitol in Washington to his partner **Alfred Vail** in Baltimore, about 40 miles away, over the first long-distance telegraph line in the United States.

The code they used wasn't quite the one you're learning. **American Morse** had dots, dashes of two lengths, and spaces *inside* some letters. Within a few years the telegraph had spread along railways across the country, and operators were reading messages by ear from the click of a sounder long before anyone thought of using a tone.
